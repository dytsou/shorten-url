How to use Shorten URL

Create a short link from a destination URL in a few steps.

  1. Enter the full destination URL, including https:// or http://.
  2. Optionally enter a custom slug. The URL prefix shown beside the field is part of your short link. Leave the field blank to generate a short key automatically.
  3. Select Shorten URL.
  4. Use Copy link to copy the result, or Visit to open it.
